Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 — M Fintech Solutions

Please note: this is a general-purpose starting template, not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review and adapt this — especially if you'll handle real client GST data — before relying on it for a live product.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, hashed password, GSTIN, business name, state code
  • Conversion history: file names, invoice counts, GST period, and the generated JSON output
  • Basic technical logs (e.g. for rate limiting and security)

2. What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your invoice data with third parties except as required to operate the Service itself (e.g. sending you account emails via our email provider).

3. How data is stored

Data is stored in a database operated on behalf of M Fintech Solutions. Passwords are hashed and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. Reasonable technical measures (encryption in transit via HTTPS, access controls) are used to protect your data, though no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. Email communications

We send account-related emails (email verification, password reset). These are functional, not marketing, emails tied directly to actions you take.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, request correction of inaccurate data, or request deletion of your account and associated data, by contacting M Fintech Solutions through your usual support channel.

6. Data retention

Conversion history is retained until you delete individual entries or request full account deletion.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to M Fintech Solutions through your usual support channel.
